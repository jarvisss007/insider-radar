**Why the 09-05 restatement missed them is the transferable part.** That audit's criterion was "priced **below** the entry day's low". A prior-session close lands **above** the day's high exactly when the stock **fell** that day — so a one-sided audit is blind to the same defect on the other side, and blind in a **directionally biased** way: the rows it cannot see are systematically the down days. RVSB and YORW are those rows. `price_audit.py` tests both edges for that reason.
