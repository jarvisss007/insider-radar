## ⚠️ Council follow-up: the SCTX number is wrong, and so is the field it came from
The directive asked me to verify SCTX ($216.2M, logged 07-31) before 08-30. Verified
against the 07-31 snapshot and the underlying accessions. It does not hold:

- That snapshot carried **61 SCTX purchase rows for 4 unique (accession, insider) filings.**
  Raw sum = $216,203,418.75. **Deduplicated sum = $35,144,261.25 — the thesis is 6.15×
  overstated.**
- The real cluster is also a different *kind* of event than the thesis implies: three of
  the four are a **$15.00 primary placement on 07-27** (ORBIMED ADVISORS $15.0M,
  GORDON CARL L $15.0M, AH BIO FUND II $5.0M), not open-market conviction buying. The
  only open-market-looking buy is Aghazadeh Behzad, $144,266 at $18.25 on 07-28.
- **XAIR confirmed at 123×**: thesis says $27.7M; the actual two filings are
  $199,998.72 (CEO) + $24,998.40 (CFO) = **$224,997.12**. 27.7M / 224,997 = 123.1 —
  i.e. exactly ~123 duplicate copies of the same two Form 4s.

**Root cause**: cluster `total_value` sums raw purchase rows without collapsing repeated
(accession, insider) transactions. This is live, not historical — today's feed is
**76 rows for 53 unique (acc, insider) pairs, 15 keys duplicated, some 4×**. The
collector's `seen`/`have_acc` guard (collector_edgar.py:216) stops an accession being
re-*fetched*, but each fetch can emit several identical transaction rows, and nothing
dedupes downstream of that.

**Consequence for this lab's hypotheses**: `total_value` is not comparable across
tickers or over time, because the inflation factor depends on how many rows a filing
happened to spawn. The "big-dollar clusters score better" idea in lessons.md is
**untestable on this field** until it is deduplicated. Insider *counts* look unaffected
(they come from a set of names, collector_edgar.py:196).

Per AGENT.md and the council directive, the SCTX/XAIR **scoring fields are untouched** —
prices and check dates stand and those rows will score honestly. It is the thesis text
that must not be read as conviction. Today's FUNC row states the deduplicated figure.
