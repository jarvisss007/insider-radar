    Base rates — what IPOs do on average (the boring truth)

    How to read this. The mean is positive and the median is negative: a handful of huge winners drag the average up while the typical IPO loses money in year one from the day-1 close. Buying the close of a hot debut is, systematically, the worst entry in the dataset. If there is an edge anywhere in IPOs, the literature puts it in getting allocations at the offer price — which retail almost never does — not in chasing the open.
